02 · The question

Four decisions, one address.

Most people choose a city on a feeling and a tax rate they read on a forum. Then they spend 5 years fixing the structure they never designed. We model the whole thing at once: the jurisdiction, the passport, the entity, and the life. Because the right answer for a founder with an exit is the wrong answer for an executive on salary, and neither is the answer for a family that needs a school and a direct flight home.

Model note. Tax figures use a simplified marginal model with indicative 2026 headline brackets, ignore social contributions, most deductions, wealth taxes, and treaties, and are not advice. Net HNWI inflow figures are country level, from the Henley Private Wealth Migration Report 2025, which projected 142,000 millionaire relocations in 2025 and 165,000 in 2026.
