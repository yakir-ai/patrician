09 · Questions

Asked before every move.

Is this tax or legal advice?

No. Patrician is a modeling and introduction service. The engine gives you an indicative ranking on simplified 2026 rules. The dossier turns that into a decision brief and connects you to licensed counsel in the jurisdiction you choose. Counsel signs off, we do not.

How accurate is the tax model?

It uses headline marginal brackets per jurisdiction, treats US citizens as taxed worldwide, flags special regimes rather than applying them, and ignores social contributions, most deductions, wealth taxes, and treaties. It is built to rank, not to file. The dossier models your case properly.

What exactly is in the $149 Dossier?

About 40 pages on your exact profile: modeled tax across your top 3 on your income and your exit, the realistic visa or residence path for the passports you hold, the entity and holding questions to settle before you move, cost of the life, schools, housing corridors, flight times, and the 5 questions to put to counsel before you sign anything. Read a sample.

How fast is delivery?

The Dossier is delivered within 72 hours of payment. The Architecture takes 10 working days because counsel reviews it. The Move is by application and starts with a call.

What if it does not help?

If the Dossier does not change how you think about the decision, say so within 7 days of delivery and it is refunded.

Why is my city ranked where it is?

The ranking is your weights applied to 8 axes: net kept over 10 years, safety, schools, sun, distance from home, beauty, cost, and ease of entry. Move a slider and the order changes. Two people with the same income get different answers, which is the point.

Where does the migration data come from?

Net millionaire inflow figures are country level, from the Henley Private Wealth Migration Report 2025, which projected 142,000 millionaire relocations in 2025 and 165,000 in 2026. Visa thresholds and tax brackets are indicative 2026 figures and change often. Verify before acting.

Who is behind Patrician?

A product operator who spent 12 years building growth systems in fintech and SaaS, and got tired of watching friends choose a country on a forum post and a feeling. The desk exists to replace that with a model, then a conversation with the right lawyer.
