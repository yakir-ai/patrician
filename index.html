09 · Questions

What people ask before they move.

Straight answers. If yours is not here, the desk replies within 24 hours at hello@patrician.ch.

Is this tax or legal advice?

No. Patrician is a modeling and introduction service. The engine gives you an indicative ranking on simplified 2026 rules. The dossier turns that into a decision brief and connects you to licensed counsel in the jurisdiction you choose. Counsel signs off, we do not.

How accurate is the tax model?

It uses headline marginal brackets per jurisdiction, treats US citizens as taxed worldwide, flags special regimes rather than applying them, and ignores social contributions, most deductions, wealth taxes, and treaties. It is built to rank, not to file. The dossier models your case properly.

What exactly is in the $149 Dossier?

About 40 pages on your exact profile: modeled tax across your top 3 on your income and your exit, the realistic visa or residence path for the passports you hold, the entity and holding questions to settle before you move, cost of the life, schools, housing corridors, flight times, and the 5 questions to put to counsel before you sign anything. Read a sample.

How fast is delivery?

The Dossier is delivered within 24 hours of payment. The Architecture takes 10 working days because counsel reviews it. The Move is by application and starts with a call.

What if it does not help?

If the Dossier does not change how you think about the decision, say so within 7 days of delivery and it is refunded.

Why is my city ranked where it is?

The ranking is your weights applied to 8 axes: net kept over 10 years, safety, schools, sun, distance from home, beauty, cost, and ease of entry. Move a slider and the order changes. Two people with the same income get different answers, which is the point.

Where does the data come from?

One number on the card comes from a single outside source: the net millionaire flow, Henley & Partners' annual estimate of millionaires moving in and out of each country. It is one line per country, so it sits on the card as context, not as a score.

Everything the engine ranks on is built here. Tax is computed from each jurisdiction's own statutory brackets, surcharges, and inbound regimes, applied to your income and your exit, which is why Zug and Zurich give different answers and a headline rate never appears. The visa doors are read from each program's current rules and thresholds, jurisdiction by jurisdiction. Cost of the life, safety, schools, and sun hours are indexed against Zurich at 100 from public records. The wealth signals, watch houses, supercar dealers, and the street index, are scored by us, city by city, from what is actually on the ground.

All of it is indicative for 2026 and changes often. Counsel verifies before you act, which is what the Dossier arranges.

Who is behind Patrician?

A private desk, not a firm. It exists because the people who can live anywhere get the worst advice: a banker who wants the assets in his booking center, an agent who earns on the property, a program that pays whoever brought you in. Patrician sells none of those. We build the model on your numbers, put counsel in the jurisdiction you choose across the table, and step back. Discretion is the product. No client is named, no case is published, and the desk takes a small number of engagements at a time so each one gets the person who built it.
