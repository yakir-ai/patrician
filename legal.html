When you request a dossier or subscribe to the letter we receive what you type: name, email address, income band, the jurisdiction you selected, the desk you chose, and the inputs you set in the engine (income, liquidity event, passports held, household, home base). We use this to reply to you, to prepare the work you asked for, and to send the letter if you subscribed. The engine itself runs entirely in your browser; sliders and selections are not transmitted unless you submit a form.
