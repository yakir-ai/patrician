How much tax would a $1M earner pay in Cancún?

On the simplified 2026 model, about $335K, an effective 34%, leaving $665K. At $300K the rate is 30%, at $3M it is 35%. Social contributions, deductions, and treaties are not included.

How does a foreigner get residence in Cancún?

Temporary residence on proof of income or assets, permanent residence after 4 years or directly with a higher asset threshold. Residents are taxed on worldwide income, there is no inbound regime. Routes that matter for money: Golden visa, Remote work visa.
