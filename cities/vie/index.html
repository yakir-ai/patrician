How much tax would a $1M earner pay in Vienna?

On the simplified 2026 model, about $482K, an effective 48%, leaving $518K. At $300K the rate is 44%, at $3M it is 53%. Social contributions, deductions, and treaties are not included.

How does a foreigner get residence in Vienna?

EU nationals register on arrival. Others need the Red-White-Red card, usually with a job offer or as a key worker. No investment route. Routes that matter for money: Founder & talent.
