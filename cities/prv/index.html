Questions
How much tax would a $1M earner pay in Provence?

On the simplified 2026 model, about $454K, an effective 45%, leaving $546K. At $300K the rate is 39%, at $3M it is 47%. Social contributions, deductions, and treaties are not included. Special regime: Impatriate regime: 30% of employment income exempt for up to 8 years for people hired from abroad, and no wealth tax on financial assets, only on property above €1.3M

How does a foreigner get residence in Provence?

EU nationals register on arrival. Others use the Talent Passport for founders, investors, and senior hires. The impatriate regime is elected on arrival, not later. Routes that matter for money: Golden visa, Founder & talent.
