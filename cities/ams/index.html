Who gets in

The residence route.

EU nationals register on arrival. Americans use the Dutch American Friendship Treaty, self employed residence on €4,500 of capital. Others use the highly skilled migrant permit through a recognized sponsor, the startup visa, or the self employed route. Permanent residence after 5 years.

Questions
How much tax would a $1M earner pay in Amsterdam?

On the simplified 2026 model, about $484K, an effective 48%, leaving $516K. At $300K the rate is 46%, at $3M it is 49%. Social contributions, deductions, and treaties are not included. Special regime: The 30% ruling: 30% of salary tax free for 5 years for hires from abroad, stepping to 27% from 2027 and capped at the public sector pay norm. Box 3 taxes a deemed return on savings and investments, which works like a wealth tax of roughly 2% a year

How does a foreigner get residence in Amsterdam?

EU nationals register on arrival. Americans use the Dutch American Friendship Treaty, self employed residence on €4,500 of capital. Others use the highly skilled migrant permit through a recognized sponsor, the startup visa, or the self employed route. Permanent residence after 5 years. Routes that matter for money: Founder & talent, Golden visa.
