Who gets in

The residence route.

British and Irish citizens need no visa, but housing is licensed: a newcomer buys or rents a home only with a licence, and High Value Residency is the licence for wealth. Everyone else applies through the same route or a licensed job. No capital gains tax, no inheritance tax, and 1 hour from London.
